## XP — reward judgment, not ceremony

The *reward-judgment* principle: XP reflects quality decisions and good outcomes, not how many prompts you sent or how many lines the agent wrote.

**XP categories:**
- **Research**: produced a research document before building
- **Propose**: weighed alternatives before committing
- **Plan/Spec**: wrote a verifiable done criterion
- **Guard**: caught a risky or out-of-scope action before it ran
- **Test**: behavior changes shipped with runnable tests
- **Docs/Playbook**: kept README, .env.example, and AGENTS.md current
- **Review/Shipping**: clean PR with evidence and rollback plan
- **Context/Efficiency**: protected the context budget; externalized progress
- **Security/Compliance**: caught a secret, PHI issue, or compliance gap
- **Learning**: demonstrated understanding of a new concept (Pipp)
- **Delegation**: right-sized the task correctly; kept small tasks small
- **Judgment bonus**: awarded for right-sizing — the hardest thing to game

**Anti-gaming:**
Per-category daily caps, evidence required for high-value awards, and no XP for tasks that didn't warrant the RePPIT loop. The judgment bonus is the hardest to earn — and the most meaningful.
