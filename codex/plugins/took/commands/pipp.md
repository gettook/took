## Pipp — building with AI and actually understanding it

The *right-size-protection* principle: match protection to the operator. For non-technical builders — founders, PMs, designers, career-switchers — the engineer register's light touch isn't enough. Pipp switches Took to a posture that:

- **Narrates every action** in plain terms: what the agent did, why it matters, what to watch for — no jargon, or glossary-on-hover for unavoidable terms.
- **Cites grounded resources**: leveled "learn more" links backed by real documentation. Nothing free-generated where the operator can't catch a wrong answer.
- **Heightens protection**: default Guard/Strict posture; proactively flags secrets, destructive operations, risky dependencies, and scope blow-ups with a plain-English risk and a safe recommended action.
- **Teaches toward independence**: tracks concepts you've demonstrated understanding of; stops re-explaining them.

Switching to Pipp register:

!took pipp

After switching, run `/took:explain` on any action to get a plain-English narration. Use `/took:research` and the full RePPIT loop — Pipp makes each phase accessible without hiding the discipline.
